Why students choose NAU

Community, purpose, and opportunity.

At Northern Arizona University, you’ll find more than a campus, you’ll find community, purpose, and opportunity. With career-driven programs, real-world experience, and a stunning Flagstaff location, NAU offers a college journey that’s as affordable as it is unforgettable. Your education made affordable

Opportunity should be within reach.

Cost shouldn’t be a barrier to opportunity. That’s why we offer competitive tuition, generous scholarships, and Arizona resident tuition for qualified nonresidents. NAU students graduate with less debt than the national average.
